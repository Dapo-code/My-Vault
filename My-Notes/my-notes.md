# My Notes Folder Guide

Use the `My-Notes` folder to store regular working notes, drafts, and day-to-day references that are not vault-level control files.

## What Goes In Here
- Daily notes
- Meeting notes
- Project working notes
- Temporary capture notes that may later be refined

## What Should Stay Outside
- Vault governance and maintenance notes (for example: `Oladapo.md`, `changelog.md`)
- Skill definitions that control index or changelog behavior
